## Summary
Carnivores do NOT go extinct by tick 100 for seed 12345 (4 alive at tick 100; the failing assertion is scavenger, the sibling issue). The carnivore founders are on a net-negative energy budget: kills are rare, they almost never reproduce, and their founders sit at E≈7–10 at tick 100, so extinction is ~10–30 ticks away.

## Proximate cause
(b)+(c)+(a) combined, not a single gate: an energy deficit. Successful feeding is rare, and two priority rules (flee-from-fellow-predator, drink-preempts-feeding) suppress hunting for roughly 1/3 of carnivore-ticks. With near-zero surplus, founders never reproduce, so the lineage collapses on the starvation clock.
The reproduction gate (firstBirthUrgency, engine.ts ~994) is NOT the carnivore cause: carnivores are not on the firstBirthUrgency path (only scavenger or timing.urgency>0), and the founders are below the energy threshold, not blocked by the gate.

## Suggested fix (for follow-up issue, NOT yet tested)
Primary candidate (test before shipping): creature.ts decideTick, ~line 466–470. Let predators eat before drinking when prey is adjacent or in reach:
```
- if (isDehydrated && canDrink) {
+ if (isDehydrated && canDrink &&
+     !((creature.traits.energyStrategy === 'carnivore' || creature.traits.energyStrategy === 'omnivore')
+       && scan.foodCreatures.length > 0 && creature.energy < creature.traits.size * MAX_ENERGY_MULTIPLIER * 0.5)) {
    decision = 'drink';
```
Rationale: hydration gates only until drink; this lets hungry predators with visible prey hunt first. Needs a before/after run of defaultOpening.test.ts (all seeds) and the carnivore trace.
Secondary: consider not counting same-strategy carnivores as flee threats (Experiment A) only together with the drink change; A alone was negative.
