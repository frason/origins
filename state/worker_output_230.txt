## Summary
Implemented Phase 0 equilibrium-progress meter (`EquilibriumTracker`) that tracks ecosystem progress toward equilibrium by monitoring Order/Chaos/Exploration/Replacement conditions simultaneously over a 2,000-tick streak. Reuses existing ecosystemHealth scoring; handles intervention resets, pause freezing, and readyForCompletion signal per SPEC.md rules.
