## Why option (a) — bit-identical restore — was ruled out
The outcome delta is NOT caused by the tie-break sort. Measured control at tick 30
(same seeds): sorted vs. unsorted differ only trivially (e.g. totalBiomass
326390 vs 326389; occupiedTileCount 21 vs 22). The delta vs. the old pin comes
from the decide/execute phase split + per-entity RNG streams added in c54e1a8.
Restoring pre-#173 output would require reverting that architecture, which
rngStreamIsolation.test.ts and decisionIntent.test.ts depend on.

## Why the old order was wrong (now documented at engine.ts Phase 1)
- Iterated `creatures` in array-construction order: determinism was an artifact
  of array layout, not a simulation invariant.
- Decided AND executed inline per creature: creature N saw creature N-1's
  already-applied move — systematic intra-tick first-mover advantage.
